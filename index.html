How it works

No forms, no folders, no setting due dates. Just talk to it.

Brandle it

The second a thought pops up, say it or type it. BrindleBrandle files it for you: work or home, quick or big, where and when.

Brindle when you're ready

Tell it how much energy you have. You get one suggestion that fits your time, your place and your tank. Not a list. One thing.

Let it go

Done? Tap it. Not now? Skip it, guilt-free. Every so often, swipe through a few cards to keep, snooze or drop.

Made for brains that never stop

If your mind runs a hundred tabs at once, regular to-do apps can feel like one more thing to manage. Big lists turn into guilt. Deciding what to do next becomes its own chore.

BrindleBrandle started as a habit on one person's phone: say one word to capture a thought, another to get unstuck. It worked, so it became an app. It's built with ADHD in mind, and it's kind to anyone with a full plate.
